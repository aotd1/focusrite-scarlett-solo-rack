This remix adapts the opening to a measured Scarlett Solo 4th Gen body without
rubber feet, adds the central lower lip and sleeve ventilation, reduces rear
side-wall thickness, and retains the original 84.2 mm depth and rack-slot profiles.
The knob variant mirrors the layout and adds a closed Ø40.8 × 13 mm cup with a
rear Ø6 × 2 mm magnet recess. The centered options relocate the sleeve to the
panel center; one adds six 6 × 26 mm front slots.
